# Discount and approval policy (FY26)

| Discount off list | Who approves | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account executive | Immediate |
| Over 10% up to 20% | Regional sales director | 1 business day |
| Over 20% up to 30% | Deal desk review board | 3 business days |
| Over 30% | Not permitted without a competitive-displacement exception from the VP of sales | 5 business days |

## Term incentives

- 48-month Flex terms get an extra 3% off monthly reserve pricing.
- 60-month terms get an extra 5%. Term incentives stack with the negotiated discount,
  and the approval level is decided by the combined figure.

## Rules

- Usage above reserve (`GL-BLK-BURST-TIB`) is never discounted.
- One-time services (`SVC-INSTALL`) may be discounted up to 50% with AE approval when
  bundled with a 36-month or longer Flex term.
- Quotes are valid for 30 days.

# Employee handbook (US), 2026 edition

## 3.1 Paid time off

- Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period: 15 days a year in years 0 to 4,
  20 days in years 5 to 9, and 25 days from year 10.
- Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year; the rest is forfeited on
  December 31.
- PTO is requested in Workday at least 2 weeks ahead for absences over 3 days.

## 3.2 Sick leave

- 10 paid sick days a year, separate from PTO, not carried over. More than 3
  consecutive sick days needs a doctor's note.

## 3.4 Parental leave

- 16 weeks of fully paid leave for any parent after birth, adoption or foster
  placement, taken within 12 months of the event, in up to two blocks.
- Eligibility starts after 6 months of employment.
- Tell your manager and file in Workday at least 30 days before the leave starts when
  the date is foreseeable.

## 4.2 Business travel

- Book through the travel portal (Concur). Economy for flights under 6 hours; premium
  economy allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Hotels up to the city rate in the portal; the default cap is $250 a night.
- Meals: per diem of $85 a day in the US.

## 4.3 Expenses

- Submit expenses in Concur within 30 days with itemised receipts for anything over $25.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only at customer events with a customer present.
- Expenses over 90 days old are not reimbursed.

## 5.1 Hybrid work

- Employees in hybrid roles work from a Contoso office at least 3 days a week.
- Fully remote arrangements need VP approval and are reviewed yearly.
- Working from another country for more than 10 working days a year needs HR and tax
  approval in advance.
